## OQ-03 — What are the turn rules (clock, start condition, sudden death)?

- **Status**: answered
- **Raised**: 2026-11-04 by cpt-interview
- **Blocks**: turn manager
- **Context**: Spec is silent; the GunBound reference uses a 60 s clock. The default was ours until the client set it.
- **Assumption**: sequential turns, 20 s clock, host starts once both teams are ready, no sudden-death cap, all values in

  first expiry. This replaces both the earlier 30 s default and GunBound's own 60 s (GB's clock is at
  `CONSTANTS.md#timing`); at 4v4 a round therefore stays inside a 3–8 minute match.

## OQ-19 — What are the missing match rules in the reference?

- **Status**: answered
- **Raised**: 2026-11-04 by cpt-interview
- **Blocks**: matches, damage, wind
- **Context**: The genre fixes the shape of a match (turn timer, slots, turn order) but not the numbers: HP, the
  blast-damage curve, the wind model and the weapon list had to be decided before matches could be sized.
- **Assumption**: HP 100, radius damage, wind ±10 units changing per turn (see OQ-02, OQ-03).
- **Answer**: answered 2026-11-04 from the genre rules the client described, recorded in
  [`02-reference/gameplay/reference-gunbound.md`](../02-reference/gameplay/reference-gunbound.md) (rewritten 2026-11-05
  without any third-party source, `CR-2026-11-05-no-decompile`). The genre gives the *rules*: turn order is a **delay
  queue** (a base delay per mobile plus a delay term per item, sorted by accumulated delay), each mobile has **weapon 1, weapon 2 and one super shot** with power/shape/density/attribute in **Inner/Mid/Outer**
  tiers, health is **HP + shield + regen + defense power**, a turn can include **movement** (direction, run flag, bounded
  by move distance and max incline, paid in **Energy**), there are **11 battle items** with a Delay cost each, **4 modes**
  (Solo/Score/Tag/Jewel), up to **11 maps** with per-map gimmicks (Force/Gust/Electric/Wind/Return/Heal/Fog) and room
  capacity is **4/6/8**. The genre does **not** hand us an integrator or any damage/HP/wind constants, so those are ours
  and are fixed by `tunables.md` and one calibration shot. Whether to adopt the rules is
  `OQ-36`.

## OQ-24 — Does the snapshot budget survive 8 players?

- **Status**: answered
- **Raised**: 2026-11-04 by cpt-interview
- **Blocks**: netcode stories, load test
- **Context**: Spec's < 500 B snapshot and 60 FPS targets were written for 2–4 players; 8 players roughly doubles state
  volume per packet.
- **Assumption**: snapshot ≤ 800 B with quantised positions, 10 Hz state at 8 players, verified in P5.
- **Answer**: Host-star mesh keeps the links at n−1 (max 7) instead of 28, so snapshots are relayed only by the host: at 8
  players ≈ 7 × 300 B × 15 Hz ≈ 31 KB/s up (ADR-0004). Quantised positions and 10 Hz state at 8 players hold the
  per-peer rate; the 8-player uplink is measured in the P5 load test, which is the remaining risk.
