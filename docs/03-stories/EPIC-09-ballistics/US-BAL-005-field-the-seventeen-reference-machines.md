# US-BAL-005 — Field the seventeen reference machines

## 1. Story

**As a** player

**I want** the seventeen machines of the original, each with its own weight and wind appetite

**so that** picking a machine is a decision about trajectory, not about a stat sheet.

## 2. Context

We have twenty machines of our own, none of which has a weight or a wind drag, and each of which caps its own power at a
number between 92 and 120 of an arbitrary scale. Under the original formula the weight *is* the gravity and the drag *is*
the wind sensitivity, and the bar is 0–4 for everyone. Three of our rows duplicated a profile another row already had.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

| # | Given | When | Then |
|---|-------|------|------|
| AC1 | the roster data | it is loaded | exactly **17** machines, each with `weight` (4.20–7.50) and `windDrag` (0.0403–0.0950) from the reference, and the mapping table in § 4 is the one implemented |
| AC2 | the three dropped machines (`mbl-06` Silver Ibex, `mbl-08` Volcano Toad, `mbl-16` Sun Lion) | the data, loadout screen, i18n and tests are searched | no reference to them remains, and the loadout screen offers 17 cards |
| AC3 | any machine | it is fired | the bar is 0–4; `maxPower` no longer exists in the type, the data or the HUD |
| AC4 | the machines that map to a reference row | they are compared | they keep our names, hull, weapons, angles, HP, shield, energy and effects; only gravity and wind sensitivity come from the mapped row |
| AC5 | Boomer's counterpart | wind is read | its wind coefficient is `2.065 × (0.222 / 0.046) = 9.966`, i.e. about twice the mid-weight machines' |
| AC6 | a match record or a marks screen | it names a machine | the id is stable across the drop — no surviving machine changes its `mobileId` |

## 5. Rules and edge cases

- A dropped id in a stored loadout or reference is refused with a readable message and the seat falls back to the first
  machine of the list, never to `undefined`.
- The dropped machines' marks, statistics and favourite-machine records are deleted with them; the migration is explicit in
  the D1 schema rather than left to a null check.
- `weight` and `windDrag` are required in the type, so a missing row fails the build rather than the match.
- The roster is not sorted differently: the loadout keeps its current order minus the three removed cards.

## 6. Implementation notes

- `packages/core/src/data/mobiles.ts` is the single source; `docs/02-reference/data/mobiles.md` § 1–§ 3 prints the same
  table and is regenerated by hand in the same commit.
- The i18n keys for the three dropped machines are removed from both locales, and the bundle budget is re-measured (it
  should fall).

## 7. Out of scope

- New machines, skins or factions.
- Balance changes to HP, damage or effects — those stay as they are.
